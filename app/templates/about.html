About BD DataHub

Find Bangladesh data in one place.

Public data about Bangladesh is spread across dozens of ministry, department, central bank and international websites. BD DataHub brings the information about those datasets into one searchable catalog, so students, researchers, journalists, developers, analysts, policymakers and citizens can find what exists, check who publishes it, and go straight to the original source.

What we do — and don't do

  • We catalogue metadata: publisher, description, source URL, coverage period, formats, update frequency and geographic level.
  • We link to the original publisher for every dataset. We do not host, redistribute or modify the data itself.
  • We prioritise official sources: .gov.bd ministries, divisions and departments, Bangladesh Bank, statutory bodies and other public institutions, followed by international organizations that publish Bangladesh-specific data.

Where the data comes from

We don't generate, create or change any data ourselves. Every dataset here is published by its original source: a government ministry, department, public institution or international organization.

BD DataHub only gathers these sources and gives you a direct link to each one, so you can find everything in one place instead of searching dozens of separate websites.

Responsible source monitoring

Our link checker reads only publicly accessible pages. It identifies itself with a descriptive User-Agent, follows robots.txt, spaces out requests to each site, and downloads no more than a small capped amount of each page. It uses that to confirm the link works and to spot new or removed documents, never to copy datasets.

Data use

Datasets belong to their publishers and come under the publishers' own terms. Where a license is stated at the source, we record it; otherwise we say so. Always check the original site before you reuse or redistribute data, and cite the publisher.
